# ClothesAI

## Vision
A fully local, network-free mobile application that allows users to digitize their wardrobe by taking photos of their clothes, categorize them, and get AI-powered outfit recommendations.

## Target Audience
Casual everyday users who want an easy way to organize and combine their physical wardrobe.

## Core Principles
- **Privacy First**: Everything is local. No cloud databases, no network requirements.
- **Local Multi-User**: Supports multiple local profiles without requiring online authentication.
- **AI-Powered**: Uses on-device AI (or heuristic logic) to recommend outfit combinations.
- **Visual Interface**: Image-based lists to mix and match items easily.
